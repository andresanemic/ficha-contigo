<a id="english"></a>

**Ficha Contigo is a local prototype that makes patient-granted clinical record access visible, scoped and reviewable.**

> **Its unit is the part of the record the patient allowed, for a purpose, until a time.**

All people, institutions and records are fictional; no real health or identifying data is present. The agreement, workflow and boundaries are explained in [`docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md`](./docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md).

**Why.** When a person gives clinical data to an institution, it can be hard for them to see who asked for which part, for what purpose, and what was accessed. An urgent situation can also leave the patient unable to answer at the time. Ficha Contigo explores a clear boundary: a professional's reason alone does not grant access. [`docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md`](./docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md) explains the patient-granted scope and advance emergency mandate.

**If you are judging Find Your Way or Meridian, start here.**

1. **Read the agreement.** [`docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md`](./docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md) sets out the patient-granted rules.
2. **Follow one request.** [`docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md`](./docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md#a-request-from-start-to-finish) walks through the ordinary and emergency paths.
3. **Open the evidence.** [`docs/EVIDENCE.md`](./docs/EVIDENCE.md) reports the suite and recorded walkthrough.
4. **Read the limits.** [`docs/LEGAL_AND_LIMITS.md`](./docs/LEGAL_AND_LIMITS.md) records the legal context and open questions.
5. **Check what remains unverified.** [`docs/EVIDENCE.md`](./docs/EVIDENCE.md#current-suite) and [`docs/LEGAL_AND_LIMITS.md`](./docs/LEGAL_AND_LIMITS.md#what-the-project-does-not-establish) explain the current status and limits.

## In one minute

The fictional request, its checks, receipts and emergency review are shown in [`docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md`](./docs/HOW_IT_WORKS.md#a-request-from-start-to-finish).

## Why Ficha Contigo

In the intended model, the clinical record stays with the institution; this local prototype does not connect to one. The agreement document explains the access rules, and [`docs/LEGAL_AND_LIMITS.md`](./docs/LEGAL_AND_LIMITS.md) lists what the prototype does not establish.

**Ficha Contigo es un prototipo local de acceso a fichas clínicas autorizado por pacientes.**
